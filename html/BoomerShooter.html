Untitled Boomer Shooter

About the project

Untitled Boomer Shooter is a single-player, retro-inspired first-person shooter that I am developing in Unity as a personal project. It is my attempt to better execute the idea behind Dual Wield, my university group project: a shooter where you hold a weapon in each hand, and the pairing you choose matters more than either gun on its own. The game is in early development, so everything shown here is a work-in-progress prototype rather than a finished product.

Revisiting the idea

Unlike my other projects, this one isn't a university assignment, which gives me the freedom to return to Dual Wield and fix what our playtesters told us didn't work. Each hand now fires on its own button while sharing a single aim point, and every weapon tracks its own ammo instead of drawing from a shared pool, which was the most criticised system in the original. Every pair of weapons, including two of the same, has its own combo effect, such as firing a shotgun through your own flamethrower to set every pellet alight.

Progress so far

So far I have built a playable prototype mission with six combat arenas, six weapons, 21 weapon combinations and four enemy types. As a solo developer I am also responsible for the planning, so I have written design and technical documents, broken the work into small tasks, and play the game regularly to keep its scope realistic.
